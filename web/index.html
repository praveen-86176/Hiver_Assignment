Architecture, EDA & Design Decisions

System design rationale, data-driven brand justification for @AppleSupport, and comparison against alternative architectures.

Brand Selection: @AppleSupport

We conducted full exploratory data analysis across 108 brands in Kaggle's 2.8M-tweet dataset. @AppleSupport was selected based on 4 empirical criteria:

  • Highest Resolved Pairs: 74,613 thread pairs (highest in dataset).
  • English Purity: 93.8% English messages (vs. 87.4% for AmazonHelp).
  • Technical Diagnostic Richness: Rich spectrum spanning OS update glitches, hardware defects, battery degradation, and account security.
  • Concise Tone: Average reply length of 118 chars, fitting Twitter constraints perfectly.

Architecture & Deterministic Guardrails

Why deterministic routing instead of LLM routing?

  • Zero Routing Inference Cost: 100% of routing decisions run via policy rules in <1ms.
  • Auditable Compliance: Account lockouts, financial refunds, and legal threats are strictly routed to humans with zero hallucination risk.
  • Sublinear TF Retrieval: 15,000-feature TF-IDF index retrieves top-3 matching resolutions in <30ms.
  • LLM Grounded Drafting: The LLM is only called for auto-handle drafts, grounded with retrieved past resolutions.
